Marketplace modules for PrestaShop

Sell your PrestaShop catalogue on the marketplaces your customers already use. Each module is a separate product with its own licence, and each runs entirely inside your own shop — your product, order and customer data never passes through our servers.

The modules

One module per marketplace. They can be installed side by side in the same shop, and neither depends on the other.

Amazon Marketplace Pro

Publish your catalogue as Amazon listings with guided product-type profiles, synchronise prices and stock, import Amazon orders (merchant-fulfilled and FBA), track fees and competitive pricing, and automate the cycle with scheduled tasks. Supports Amazon marketplaces in Europe, North America, Japan, Australia, Singapore, India and the Middle East.

Features & pricing

eBay Marketplace Pro

Publish your catalogue as eBay listings with category and item-aspect mapping, multi-variation listings and your own business policies, synchronise prices and stock, import eBay orders and returns, track fees and seller performance. Supports 19 eBay sites across Europe, the Americas and Asia-Pacific.

Features & pricing

Data handling

Both modules run inside the merchant's own PrestaShop installation and talk to the marketplace's official API directly from the merchant's server. IntelliPresta operates only a stateless authorisation service that completes the connection handshake; it has no database and stores no product, order or buyer data. The full statement — what is collected, how it is used, stored, protected, shared, retained and deleted — is in our privacy & data handling policy, which covers both modules.
