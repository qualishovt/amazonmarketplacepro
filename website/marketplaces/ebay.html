eBay Marketplace Pro — sell on eBay sites from your own PrestaShop store

Publish your catalogue as eBay listings, keep prices and stock in sync, and import eBay orders straight into the PrestaShop back office your team already uses. Everything runs inside your own shop — your product and customer data never passes through our servers.

What the module does

eBay Marketplace Pro covers the full selling cycle: creating listings, keeping them accurate, winning the sale, and handling what comes after it.

Category mapping and item aspects

Map your PrestaShop categories to eBay categories and fill the item aspects eBay requires for each one — required and recommended fields, allowed values included — from PrestaShop features, attributes or fixed defaults.

Catalogue matching and barcodes

Match your products against the eBay catalogue so listings inherit the right product identity, and audit your catalogue for the EAN, UPC and MPN values eBay requires before a listing can go live.

Variation listings

PrestaShop combinations become eBay multi-variation listings, with per-variant prices, stock and images grouped under a single item.

Price & stock synchronisation

Per-category, per-manufacturer and per-supplier markup rules, quantity buffers and safety switches. Offers and inventory are pushed on a schedule so your eBay listings follow your shop.

Business policies

Your eBay payment, returns and shipping policies are fetched from your seller account and assigned per listing or per category, so every publication uses the policy you intended.

Order import & fulfilment

eBay orders become PrestaShop orders with your carriers, statuses and invoices. Shipment confirmations and tracking numbers flow back to eBay, with your PrestaShop carriers mapped to eBay's shipping services.

Repricing

Apply your own pricing rules against the competition — match, undercut, or hold a margin floor — with suggested prices you approve before they are sent.

Returns, fees & performance

Return requests are imported and can be actioned from PrestaShop. eBay fees are recorded per order so you see the true margin of every sale, and your seller performance metrics are surfaced in the back office.

Promotions & listing design

Synchronise eBay promotions, and render listing descriptions from your own HTML template so every item is presented in your shop's style.

Compliance built in

EU product-safety (GPSR) contact details and country of origin per manufacturer or product, and a marketplace account-deletion endpoint that acknowledges eBay's notifications as the programme requires.

Multiple eBay sites

Sell on more than one eBay site from one shop — the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Belgium, Ireland, Austria, Switzerland, Poland, Canada, Australia, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines and India.

Automation

Every recurring job — order import, stock sync, repricing, fee collection — is available as a scheduled task, so day-to-day operation needs no clicks at all.

How it works

eBay Marketplace Pro is installed like any PrestaShop module and connects to eBay through its official developer programme.

  1. Install the module in your PrestaShop back office (PrestaShop 1.6 – 8 supported).
  2. Connect your eBay account with one click. You are sent to eBay's own consent page to approve the connection — no credentials to copy, and you can revoke the authorisation from your eBay account at any time.
  3. Map your categories to eBay categories and item aspects, assign your business policies, then publish.
  4. Let the scheduled tasks run. Orders arrive in PrestaShop, stock and prices stay current, and fees collect themselves.

Where your data lives: in your own shop's database, on hosting you control. Our connection service only completes the secure authorisation handshake — it stores no product, order or buyer data. Details in our privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Which PrestaShop versions are supported?
PrestaShop 1.6 through 8.
Which eBay sites can I sell on?
The eBay sites listed above, each with its own listing language, currency and category tree.
Do I need technical knowledge to set it up?
Installation is the standard PrestaShop module upload. Connecting your eBay account is a single click plus an approval on eBay's own website. Scheduled tasks need one-time cron entries, for which the module shows ready-to-copy commands.
Does IntelliPresta see my sales or customer data?
No. The module runs entirely inside your PrestaShop installation and talks to eBay's API directly from your server. Our connection service handles only the authorisation handshake and stores nothing. See the privacy policy for the full picture.
What happens if I stop using the module?
Revoke the connection from your eBay account (or with the module's Disconnect button) and uninstall. Your eBay listings are unaffected; the data the module stored in your own database is removed on uninstall.
How do I get help?
E-mail support@intellipresta.com. We answer within one business day. Support is part of Business care, which is included for the first 12 months.
What is Business care?
It covers module updates and e-mail support. The first 12 months are included in the licence price, so there is nothing extra to pay in year one. From the second year it is charged annually and is optional.
What happens if I do not renew Business care?
Your licence does not expire and the module carries on working exactly as it did. What stops is updates and support. Since eBay changes its APIs and category requirements over time, an un-updated module will eventually fall behind those changes — so most merchants renew, but it is your decision, and you can renew later after a gap.
