IntelliPresta Marketplace Pro — sell on Amazon marketplaces from your PrestaShop store

Publish your catalogue as Amazon listings, keep prices and stock in sync, and import marketplace orders straight into the PrestaShop back office your team already uses. Everything runs inside your own shop — your product and customer data never passes through our servers.

What the module does

Marketplace Pro for PrestaShop covers the full selling cycle: creating listings, keeping them accurate, winning the sale, and handling what comes after it.

Listing creation with guided profiles

The module downloads the attribute requirements for your product category directly from the marketplace and presents them as a form — required fields, allowed values and units included. Map each attribute to a PrestaShop field, pick a value, or set a default once per profile. Variations (size, colour) are built from your existing PrestaShop combinations.

Price & stock synchronisation

Prices with per-category, per-manufacturer or per-supplier markups, scheduled sale prices, quantity buffers and safety switches. Delta exports send only what changed, respecting marketplace submission limits on large catalogues.

Order import & fulfilment

Marketplace orders become PrestaShop orders with your carriers, statuses and invoices. Shipment confirmations and tracking numbers flow back automatically. Orders you fulfil and orders fulfilled by the marketplace (FBA) are told apart and can be routed to different statuses.

Overselling protection

When a marketplace checkout is in progress but not yet paid, the module can reserve that stock in PrestaShop so no other channel sells the same unit — and releases it automatically if the purchase never completes.

Competitive pricing

Track the current featured offer and lowest price for your listings, and apply your own rules — match, undercut, or hold a margin floor — with suggested prices you approve before they are sent.

Compliance built in

EU product-safety (GPSR) contact details and country of origin per manufacturer or product, VAT-aware order import, and support for uploading your own VAT invoices for sellers enrolled in the marketplace's VAT Calculation Service.

Fees, finances & reports

Marketplace fees are recorded per order so you can see the true margin of every sale. Settlement and inventory reports are requested, downloaded and parsed for you.

Buyer communication

Send permitted, order-related messages and review requests through the marketplace's official messaging system, and file buyer replies into PrestaShop's own customer service threads.

Automation

Every recurring job — order import, stock sync, repricing, report polling, invoice upload — is available as a scheduled task, so day-to-day operation needs no clicks at all.

How it works

Marketplace Pro for PrestaShop is installed like any other module and connects to the marketplace through its official API programme.

  1. Install the module in your PrestaShop back office (PrestaShop 1.6 – 9 supported).
  2. Connect your seller account with one click. You are sent to the marketplace's own consent page to approve the connection — no credentials to copy, and you can revoke the authorisation from your seller account at any time.
  3. Map your categories to marketplace product types using guided profiles, then publish.
  4. Let the scheduled tasks run. Orders arrive in PrestaShop, stock and prices stay current, and reports collect themselves.

Where your data lives: in your own shop's database, on hosting you control. Our connection service only completes the secure authorisation handshake — it stores no product, order or buyer data. Details in our privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Which PrestaShop versions are supported?
PrestaShop 1.6 through 9. The module is tested on each supported minor version.
Which marketplaces can I sell on?
23 Amazon marketplaces: Europe (Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Poland, Sweden, Belgium, the UK, Ireland and Turkey), North America (US, Canada, Mexico), Brazil, the Middle East and Africa (the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and South Africa), Japan, Australia, Singapore and India. Each marketplace has its own listing language and currency handling.
Do I need technical knowledge to set it up?
Installation is the standard PrestaShop module upload. Connecting your seller account is a single click plus an approval on the marketplace's own website. Scheduled tasks need one-time cron entries, for which the module shows ready-to-copy commands.
Does IntelliPresta see my sales or customer data?
No. The module runs entirely inside your PrestaShop installation and talks to the marketplace's API directly from your server. Our connection service handles only the authorisation handshake and stores nothing. See the privacy policy for the full picture.
What happens if I stop using the module?
Revoke the connection from your seller account (or with the module's Disconnect button) and uninstall. Your listings on the marketplace are unaffected; the data the module stored in your own database is removed on uninstall.
How do I get help?
E-mail support@intellipresta.com. We answer within one business day. Support is written, in English, and we also answer in German, French, Italian, Spanish and Turkish, using translation assistance where needed. Support is part of Business care, which is included for the first 12 months.
What is Business care?
It covers module updates and e-mail support. The first 12 months are included in the € 209.99 licence, so there is nothing extra to pay in year one. From the second year it costs € 60 a year and is optional.
What happens if I do not renew Business care?
Your licence does not expire and the module carries on working exactly as it did. What stops is updates and support. Since Amazon changes the Selling Partner API over time, an un-updated module will eventually fall behind those changes — so most merchants renew, but it is your decision, and you can renew later after a gap.
