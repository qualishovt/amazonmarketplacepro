PrestaShop modules for marketplace selling and store automation

IntelliPresta builds modules that extend PrestaShop stores — connecting them to online marketplaces and automating the work that would otherwise be done by hand. Every module runs inside the merchant's own shop, on the merchant's own hosting.

About IntelliPresta

IntelliPresta develops and maintains commercial modules for the PrestaShop e-commerce platform. Our modules are installed in the merchant's own PrestaShop instance and run on the merchant's own infrastructure — we do not host merchants' shops, and we do not collect or store their product, order or customer data.
