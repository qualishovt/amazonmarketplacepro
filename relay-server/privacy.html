Privacy Policy — Marketplaces Pro

Last updated: July 2026

Marketplaces Pro is a PrestaShop module developed by IntelliPresta that connects a merchant's own PrestaShop store to online marketplaces such as Amazon and eBay. This policy explains what data is involved and where it lives.

The short version

Your data stays on your own server. Marketplaces Pro runs inside your PrestaShop installation. Marketplace data it processes — orders, buyer details needed for fulfillment, product listings, prices, stock levels — is stored in your own shop's database, on infrastructure you control. IntelliPresta does not receive, store, or have access to that data.

What our servers do handle

IntelliPresta operates a small authorization service (on intellipresta.com) whose only job is to complete the secure connection between your shop and the marketplace:

  • During connection, it exchanges the one-time authorization code issued by the marketplace for the access credentials of your shop, and passes them to your shop.
  • During operation, it periodically exchanges your shop's stored credential for short-lived access tokens on request from your shop.

This service is stateless: it stores no tokens, no personal data, no order or product data, and keeps no database. Credentials pass through it over encrypted (TLS) connections only for the duration of each request.

Marketplace buyer data

Order information (including buyer names and shipping addresses where the marketplace provides them) is retrieved by your shop directly from the marketplace's API solely for order fulfillment, and is stored only in your shop's own order system. Each merchant is the data controller for their own shop's data. Buyer data is never transmitted to IntelliPresta.

Account deletion notifications

Where marketplaces (such as eBay) send account-closure notifications, our endpoint acknowledges them. As we store no user data centrally, no central deletion is required; merchants are responsible for honoring deletion obligations for data held in their own shops, and the module provides tooling to assist.

Cookies and analytics

The authorization service sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
